# Problem Statement — FlyGuard AI

## Executive Summary
Illicit financial flows moved through **money-mule networks** represent one of the fastest-growing threats to global digital banking. Criminal syndicates recruit or compromise bank accounts ("money mules") to rapidly receive, split, layer, and forward stolen funds across multi-hop domestic and cross-border payment rails within minutes—long before legacy rule-based Anti-Money Laundering (AML) systems can detect or interdict them.

---

## 1. Who Has the Problem?

### Primary Stakeholders
1. **Financial Intelligence Units (FIUs) & AML Compliance Teams at Banks, Neobanks, and Payment Service Providers (PSPs)**:
   - AML investigators and BSA/AML compliance officers are overwhelmed by **90%–95% false-positive alert rates** generated by static threshold rules (e.g., *"flag any transfer over $10,000"*).
   - Analysts spend 45–90 minutes per alert manually stitching together spreadsheets of sender/receiver histories without visibility into 2-to-4 hop transaction rings.
2. **Fraud & Risk Operations Teams on Instant Payment Rails (RTP, FedNow, UPI, SEPA Instant, P2P & Crypto Off-Ramps)**:
   - As settlement times drop from days to seconds, rapid pass-through accounts empty illicit proceeds in under 60 minutes, leaving institutions with severe operational and reimbursement liability.
3. **Legitimate High-Velocity Customers (Small Businesses, Payment Aggregators, Shared Households)**:
   - Innocent small-and-medium businesses (SMBs), merchant collectors, and remittance senders frequently trigger crude velocity rules, resulting in wrongful account freezes, friction, and customer churn.
4. **Everyday Banking Consumers & Scam Victims**:
   - Victims of authorized push payment (APP) fraud, phishing, and business email compromise (BEC) lose billions annually because receiving mule funnels liquidate funds before banks can trace the network.

---

## 2. Why Does It Matter?

1. **Massive Scale of Financial Crime**:
   - According to the UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) and global financial intelligence estimates, **$800 billion to $2 trillion USD** (2%–5% of global GDP) is laundered annually, with over **90% of cyber-enabled fraud proceeds** routed through money-mule accounts.
2. **Failure of Legacy Rule-Based Engines**:
   - Modern money-mule syndicates evade static thresholds using:
     - **Structuring / Smurfing Splitters**: Breaking large sums into sub-threshold transfers.
     - **Multi-Sender Fan-In Funnels**: Aggregating small transfers from dozens of victims before a rapid wire/crypto exit.
     - **Circular Transaction Rings & Multi-Hop Layering**: Moving funds across 3–5 intermediate accounts (`A -> B -> C -> D`) to obscure provenance.
     - **Dormant-to-Burst Account Takeovers**: Seasoning an account for months before executing a high-velocity 24-hour burst.
3. **The "Black-Box AI" Regulatory Bottleneck**:
   - Even when institutions deploy machine learning, uncalibrated black-box probabilities fail regulatory scrutiny (FATF, FinCEN, EU AMLA, OCC Model Risk Management SR 11-7). Investigators cannot file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) simply because *"the neural network output 0.87"*—they need **human-interpretable feature attributions, network evidence, and counterfactual testing**.

---

## 4. How FlyGuard AI Solves This Problem

**FlyGuard AI** bridges the gap between high-precision graph/behavioral machine learning and regulatory-grade investigative explainability:

- **26 Leakage-Free Behavioral, Temporal & Graph Features**: Captures holding-time velocity (`median_holding_time_hours`), pass-through symmetry, directed PageRank centrality, and short-cycle participation to separate true mule archetypes from legitimate high-volume SMBs ("hard negatives").
- **Calibrated Multi-Stage Risk Scoring (0–100)**: Combines `XGBoost` with `SmoothIsotonicCalibrator` on scenario-isolated splits so risk scores are monotonic, calibrated, and mapped to actionable operational queues (`Low`, `Moderate`, `High`, `Critical`).
- **Per-Account TreeSHAP Explainability**: Every flagged account displays exact feature-by-feature positive and negative risk drivers alongside plain-English investigative narratives.
- **Interactive Multi-Hop Network Intelligence**: Traces 1-to-3 hop ego networks, circular rings, and shortest laundering paths between suspect accounts in real time.
- **Counterfactual "What-If" Simulation Lab**: Allows analysts to perturb account behaviors (e.g., normalizing holding times or turnover) and immediately observe how the model's calibrated risk score responds.
- **Immutable Secret-Redacting Audit Trail**: Logs all model training runs, threshold changes, case notes, and analyst triage decisions for compliance governance.
